# A Design and Implementation of Online Exhibition Application for Disabled Artists

## Author's problem identification and argument

The authors identify a significant problem faced by disabled artists: the lack of exhibition spaces and opportunities to share their work with the public. Despite growing interest in art created by disabled individuals, there are limited venues for showcasing their work, which restricts their ability to reach wider audiences and gain recognition. The authors argue that an online exhibition platform could alleviate these issues by providing a virtual space where disabled artists can display their work without the constraints of physical exhibition spaces. The proposed solution is an Android-based application that includes features like Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) to enhance accessibility for visually and hearing-impaired users. The application also incorporates a disability certification process to ensure that only authenticated disabled artists can exhibit their work. By using technologies like MySQL for database management and Firebase Storage for handling large data sizes, the application aims to create an inclusive and accessible platform for disabled artists to showcase their creativity and engage with the public. The authors acknowledge that while the application addresses some accessibility issues, it is limited in diversity and suggests future improvements to accommodate a broader range of disabilities and artistic fields.

## Introduction Author

Seung Gyeom Kim, Ha Ram Kang, Tae Hun Kim, Jun Hyeok Lee, and Won Joo Lee are affiliated with Hanyang University and Inha Technical College in Korea. They have backgrounds in computer science and applied artificial intelligence, with research interests spanning computer vision, human-computer interaction, mobile programming, web programming, and machine learning. Their collective expertise has been applied to designing and implementing an online exhibition application tailored for disabled artists, aiming to bridge the gap between these artists and the general public by leveraging technology to overcome physical and communicative barriers.
