# A Design and Implementation of Online Exhibition Application for Disabled Artists

## Author's problem identification and argument

The authors identify a significant problem faced by disabled artists: the lack of exhibition space and opportunities to share their work with the public. Despite growing interest in art created by disabled individuals, there are limited venues and platforms for these artists to display and promote their work. This lack of exposure hinders the recognition and appreciation of their art. The authors argue that an online exhibition platform could alleviate these issues by providing a virtual space for disabled artists to showcase their work. They propose an Android-based application that includes features like Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) to enhance accessibility for visually and hearing-impaired users. The application also incorporates a disability certification process to ensure that only authenticated disabled artists can exhibit their work. By using technologies like MySQL for database management and Firebase Storage for handling large data sizes, the application aims to overcome the limitations of physical exhibition spaces and facilitate better communication between disabled artists and the general public. The authors emphasize the need for a simple and cost-effective platform that accommodates the diverse needs of disabled artists across various art forms.

## Introduction Author

Seung Gyeom Kim, Ha Ram Kang, Tae Hun Kim, Jun Hyeok Lee, and Won Joo Lee are affiliated with Hanyang University and Inha Technical College in Korea. They have backgrounds in applied artificial intelligence, computer science, and engineering. Their research interests include computer vision, human-computer interaction, multimodal deep learning, operating systems, web programming, mobile programming, database design, and machine learning. Dr. Won Joo Lee, a professor at Inha Technical College, has extensive experience in computer science and engineering, having received his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. from Hanyang University.
